What the crossing labels mean

  • Monitored (SE 12th, SE 11th, SE Division & 7th): a public traffic camera can see the tracks. Our most reliable status, but camera images can lag by several minutes.
  • Predicted (NW 9th, NW 15th, NW 17th): a trackside detector north of Portland heard a train that may be heading this way. Shown as "Possible freight train · Not confirmed". Some detected trains never come this way.
  • Estimated (SE 12th between Hawthorne and Washington): inferred from traffic speeds and nearby crossings. Treat it as a hint.
  • Not monitored: we have no live train data. "No data" never means "clear".

TrainClear is not a safety device. Always obey crossing gates, lights, bells and signs, and don't use the app while driving or riding.

Common questions

A crossing showed clear, but a train was there (or the other way round)

Please tell us. Use the report buttons on the crossing in the app (you need to be within about half a mile), or email us the crossing and time. Every report helps us fix it.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

In the app: Settings → Notifications. You can pick crossings, turn MAX and Amtrak/WES alerts on for a while, and set quiet hours (10 PM–7 AM). To stop all notifications, turn them off there or in your phone's settings.

Why does TrainClear want my location?

Only while the app is open: to center the map, to show a crossing's status as you approach it, and to check you're near a crossing before you report it. Your location stays on your phone. The app works without it.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Turn off notifications in the app and uninstall it; that removes your push token. For anything else, email CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER. See the Privacy Policy for what we keep and for how long.

Which crossings are covered?

13 rail crossings in Southeast and Northwest Portland, listed in the app's Crossings tab. We're working on more.
